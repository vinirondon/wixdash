How it works

From connection to clarity
in three steps

No code, no CSV exports, no manual data entry. WixDash reads directly from your Wix account via the official API.

1
🔗

Connect your Wix account

Click "Connect Wix", log in with your Wix credentials, and authorize read access. Takes under 60 seconds. Your data stays in Wix — we only read it.

2
⚡

Your data syncs instantly

WixDash pulls your bookings, clients, services, orders, and staff in real time. Everything is organized in a clean, fast dashboard you'll actually enjoy using.

3
💼

Manage & run payroll

See who did what, calculate staff earnings by service, export reports, and track performance — all based on real data from your Wix bookings.
